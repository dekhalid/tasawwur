Your courses,
explained with drawings

Ask about any idea and get a short answer with a drawing that shows it, summarize your lecture into an illustrated study guide, and study out loud with a tutor that draws and asks you.

For iPhone and iPad · Arabic and English · No account needed

What it does

  • Draws the idea

    Ask about any idea and get a short answer with a drawing, its parts labeled.

  • Summarize with drawings

    A quick summary or a full study guide from your file, with drawings of the key ideas, checked against your lecture.

  • A voice tutor

    Study out loud in Arabic or English; the tutor draws the idea, points at each part as it explains, and ends with a quick check.

  • Your files, sorted

    One tap makes a folder per course, and "Continue from p. ..." takes you back to the page you stopped on.

  • Write on your lectures

    With the Apple Pencil on iPad or your finger on iPhone, with pen and highlighter colors, text and photos on the page.

  • Quiz me, check my steps

    Questions from your file that focus on what you missed, and step checks that show where a solution went wrong.

Who it is for

For university, high school and middle school, including Qudurat and Tahsili questions.

Plans

Start free: 3 requests and 10 voice minutes a day. Tasawwur Plus gives you more, billed weekly, monthly or every 3 months.
